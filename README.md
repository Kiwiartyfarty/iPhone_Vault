iPhone Vault v1.0 for Mac

iPhone Vault is a free, offline Mac app for copying, transferring and backing up your iPhone data. I built it because I wanted these jobs to work the way I think they should: plug in the cable, tap Trust, and you’re in control. It needs no account, and nothing runs on a schedule.

What it does

Back up your iPhone to a folder you choose, whenever you choose.
Browse any backup offline, including Photos, Messages, Notes, Contacts and the full file system.
Copy everything out of a backup into normal folders and files you can open on any computer.
Move photos both ways by previewing and picking from the camera roll, copying the whole roll to your Mac, or sending pictures from the Mac to the iPhone.
Browse media files on the phone, such as Photos (DCIM), Downloads, Books and Recordings.
See the phone at a glance, with storage, battery, iOS version and last backup date.

Make it yours

Switch the interface with one click between four looks: Neon, Synthwave, Plain dark and Light.
